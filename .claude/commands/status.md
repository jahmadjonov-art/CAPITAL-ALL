Tell the owner where things stand. He is not a programmer — no code, no jargon,
nothing he has to decode.

Read `SCORECARD.md`, `research/beliefs.md`, the last few entries of
`research/experiments.md`, `thoughts/3-unknown.md` and `thoughts/4-walls.md`.
Run `./scorecard/summary.sh`.

Then give him, in under a page:

- **Where we are** — how many experiments have been run, what we currently
  believe, and how confident that belief actually is (the evidence tier, said in
  plain words: "only tested on history" beats writing "T1").
- **Scores so far** — the average and the trend, if any exist yet.
- **What is stuck** — open walls, and what each one needs to clear.
- **What needs you** — anything genuinely waiting on a decision from him, with
  enough context to answer it without opening a file. If nothing is waiting, say
  so plainly; do not manufacture a question.
- **What happens next** — what the next `/research` session would most likely
  pick up.

Be honest about thin evidence. If three experiments have run and none survived
contact with out-of-sample data, that is the headline, not a footnote.
