# Scorecard

A record of how the work in this repository was rated, kept so that whoever
picks it up next — a future session, a different agent — starts out knowing
what has already been asked for and what has already missed.

**The scale runs 1 to 10. 10 is the best possible, 1 is the worst.** The score
is the owner's judgement after running the work; it is not a code-quality
metric and there is nothing to appeal.

Averages and trends are deliberately not written down anywhere in this file.
They are derived from the log when someone asks for them:

```bash
./scorecard/summary.sh
```

That is the same rule the archived app in `legacy/` used for its bucket
balances, and for the same reason: a stored summary can quietly drift out of
agreement with the entries underneath it, and then it is worse than useless.

---

## Standing Directives

The distilled, binding version of everything below. Each line earned its place
from a specific score. **Follow these.** If one is genuinely wrong for the task
in front of you, say so and explain why — do not silently skip it.

When feedback reveals a lasting preference, add a line here. When newer feedback
contradicts an older line, rewrite that line rather than leaving both standing.
