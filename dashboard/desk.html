Why the gap is the model, not the market

  • Settlement is not spot. Resolves on a CF Benchmarks index averaged over the final minute. Averaging cuts terminal variance, so true fair value sits closer to the extremes than this model says. Fix this first.
  • σ is backward-looking and noisy over a 15-minute horizon.
  • Drift assumed zero — defensible over minutes, still an assumption.
  • A large gap on a liquid contract is a red flag. The market knows more than this model does.

Before any of this is tradable

  • Venue. This is Kalshi-direct; the funded account is Robinhood (~4¢ round trip vs a 1¢ spread). Unresolved, and it gates everything.
  • Calibration. Log predicted probability against realised outcome. If the 30% bucket does not settle yes ~30% of the time, the gap column is noise.
  • Costs and queue. A forced taker exit costs 1.75¢ — about 1.75 round trips of profit.
