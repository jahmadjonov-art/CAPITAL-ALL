What this measures, and what it does not

Unusual here means volume against open interest. A contract trading many times the open interest that stood there at last night's close is new positioning rather than existing positions changing hands. That is the standard screen and it is the honest version of the question we can answer with free data today.

It is not "unusual versus this ticker's own normal day." That needs a history of daily option volume, which nothing here records yet. A name that trades a million contracts every single day will keep appearing; that is turnover, not news. Until a volume history exists, read this as "where is the new positioning", not "what changed today".

Open interest is yesterday's number. It is published once, after the close, and does not move during the session — which is exactly what makes the ratio mean something, but it also means a scan run before the open is describing the previous session.

Excluded: contracts that are not really options
