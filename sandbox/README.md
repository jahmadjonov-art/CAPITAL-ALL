# Sandbox

Ground that has been staked out but not built on.

Each file here names something that should exist, says why it matters and what
is already known about it, and then stops. **Any agent may pick one up without
asking.**

## Why this exists

Some work is too large to finish honestly in one session. The options then are to
produce a shallow version that looks complete, or to mark the ground and leave it
open. The second is better, and it is what the owner asked for:

> "If it's too complicated, don't try to solve all of it all at once. You can
> leave a sandbox within the sandbox that we created... This is the goal of this.
> We haven't really figured out how to fill it up. But the future agents, if you
> want to figure out the way to do this, go ahead."

**Opening a sandbox carries no penalty and is not an admission of failure.** It
is the scope-level version of "I cannot tell" — the same honesty applied to how
much got done rather than to what was found. A half-built thing presented as
finished is the failure; saying "this is the shape of it, I got this far" is not.

An empty sandbox is therefore not debt. It is a marked plot with a note on it.

## When to open one

- The task is genuinely bigger than the session, and a partial version would
  mislead whoever reads it next.
- You found something worth pursuing that is off your current thread, and
  chasing it would abandon the question you came in on.
- You needed something that does not exist yet and worked around it — **say so,
  rather than letting the gap stay invisible.**

## When not to

Do not open one to avoid work you could do now. A sandbox for something that
takes twenty minutes is a way of not doing twenty minutes of work, and a folder
full of those is noise that hides the real ground.

## What a sandbox file contains

```
# Title

**Status:** OPEN | IN PROGRESS | CLOSED
**Opened:** YYYY-MM-DD

## The goal
What should exist when this is done. Concrete enough to recognise.

## Why it matters
What stays blocked or worse while this is empty.

## What is already known
Verified facts, working endpoints, dead ends already hit. Save the next agent
the work you already did.

## What is missing
The actual gap, as specifically as you can state it.

## How you would know it is done
The condition that closes this. Without one it stays open forever.

## Notes from whoever has been here
Append, with dates. Partial progress belongs here — that is the point.
```

## Closing one

When the goal is met, set `Status: CLOSED`, note what it produced, and promote
the result into `research/beliefs.md` or `KNOWLEDGE.md` as appropriate. **Keep
the file** — how something got built, including the dead ends, is worth as much
as the fact that it now exists.
